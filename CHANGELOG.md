# Release Notes

## Pelota 0.2.0-alpha

The second alpha: a much richer tournament, new ways to control your player, matches you can
continue later and a big step up in looks.

### Highlights

- **Tournament draw like on a tournament website.** The full bracket with your path through it
  highlighted, a profile for every player, head-to-head cards with each player's chance to win
  and the tale of the tape (ranking, age, height, serve, return, rally), and the headlines of
  the tournament: upsets, comebacks, five-setters, long tiebreaks and bagels.
- **Continue your match.** Matches are saved after every point. Pick up where you left off
  with *Continue Match* in the main menu or on the tournament screen.
- **Play with keyboard and mouse.** Move with W A S D and aim with the mouse, or choose the
  keyboard-only layout with nine aim zones (Options, *Keyboard Layout*). The tutorial shows
  both.
- **Pick your controller.** In the player select menu every connected keyboard and gamepad is
  a row: move it left for player 1, right for player 2, leave it in the middle for the CPU.
  The pause menu's new *Controllers* window lets you swap mid-match, or let the CPU take over.
- **Strokes that feel right.** A new animation system times every swing to meet the ball,
  with smoother footwork and full follow-throughs. The ball now visibly spins, grips the
  court on the bounce and rolls.
- **A more realistic stadium.** Real materials everywhere: brick facade, concrete stands,
  painted steel, plastic seats, fabric and rubber on the benches. The hard court has the fine
  grain of its sand-filled paint and the scuff marks of players' shoes, under a new sky. The
  menu background is lit exactly like a match.

### Also new

- Sharp split screen on high-DPI displays.
- Players' skin, eyes, clothes and shoes look more natural.
- Music pauses and resumes instead of starting the song over.
- The serve clock only runs before a first serve.

### Fixes

- Backhands no longer swing wrong.
- Split screen runs faster: the hidden full-screen view is no longer drawn behind it.
- The player's head only turns towards things in front of them.

## Pelota 0.1.0-alpha

The first public alpha of Pelota, a free and open source 3D tennis game for Windows and Linux.

- Play a quick match against the CPU or a friend on the same computer, with split screen.
- Play through a 128-player tournament draw, or practice against a ball machine.
- Three shot buttons plus aim: time your release for the perfect shot.
- A packed US Open stadium with a chair umpire, TV graphics and broadcast camera cuts.
- Graphics presets from low to ultra, and a crowd you can thin out.

This is an early alpha: expect bugs and missing features. Please report problems at
https://github.com/mbkma/pelota/issues.
